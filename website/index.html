A voice assistant that speaks your language — offline, on any phone.

Neyam Vaani is a free, open source AI assistant for Android that runs with zero internet on a $200 phone and gives Tamil, Hindi, Bengali, Kannada, Telugu, Marathi, Bhojpuri and Maithili the same respect English gets. Speak to it — it speaks back.

What is Neyam Vaani?

Hundreds of millions of people in India own a smartphone but can't comfortably use it — because it demands typing in English, constant internet, and expensive hardware. Vaani is built on the opposite of all three assumptions. It answers questions, drafts and sends WhatsApp and SMS messages, reads letters and medicine labels aloud, translates between vernacular languages, fills government forms by voice, sets reminders, and runs phone actions — all by speaking, all on-device.

🗣️ Ask anything, by voice

"தமிழ்நாடு முதல்வர் யார்?" — ask in your language, get a short, spoken answer in your language. Powered by an on-device model, not the cloud.

✉️ Dictate, don't type

"अन्ना को WhatsApp मैसेज भेजो" — dictate messages, formal letters and school notes. Vaani drafts them naturally, you send them with one tap.

📄 Read the paper world

Photograph a letter, form or medicine label; Vaani reads it aloud and explains it in plain language — what it says and what you need to do.

Seven promises we can't break

Every design decision must satisfy all seven — codified in the project's Tender UX Principles and Privacy Charter.

📶 Offline-first

Every core feature works with zero internet. Connectivity is an upgrade, never a requirement.

📱 Cheap-hardware-first

Target device: a $200 Android phone with 2GB RAM. Cold start under 3 seconds.

🎙️ Voice-first

Voice in, voice out. Typing is a fallback for people who want it, never a requirement.

🇮🇳 Vernacular-first

Nine languages at parity — including Bhojpuri and Maithili, which commercial assistants skip.

🔒 Private by default

All AI runs on your phone. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry, no ads. Recordings are never stored.

💛 Tender UX

No dark patterns, no engagement hooks, no unrequested notifications. Errors are kind; silence is respected.

…and the test that governs them all: if a 70-year-old in a Tamil village can't use it without help, it's broken.

How it works — entirely on your phone

  1. You speak. On-device speech recognition (Whisper) transcribes and detects whether you're speaking Tamil, Hindi, or any supported language.
  2. Vaani understands. A fast classifier routes your request; an on-device language model (Qwen 2.5 via llama.cpp) handles questions, drafting and explanation — no server involved.
  3. Vaani acts & answers. Send the message, set the reminder, or answer from a local knowledge base — then it speaks the reply in your language (Piper neural voices).

Full architecture, model budget and stack rationale in the docs.

Frequently asked questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes — 100% of core features are offline. Speech recognition, the language model, translation and text-to-speech all run on the phone. The only network use is optional, opt-in language pack downloads.

What does it cost?

Nothing, ever. Apache-2.0 open source. No ads, no accounts, no premium tier — these are permanent product decisions written into the project charter.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones with as little as 2GB RAM (e.g., Redmi 9A). The app auto-selects smaller AI models on low-RAM devices and keeps its download under 80MB.

What happens to my voice data?

Voice exists in memory only and is never stored. Conversation text is encrypted on-device (SQLCipher) and deletable at any time. Nothing is ever uploaded — there is no server to upload to.

Who is building this?

Neyam OpenSc, an open source organization — with native-speaker language stewards owning quality for each language. Contributions welcome.

When can I try it?

The project is in Phase 0 of a 90-day roadmap to public beta. Watch the repository for releases.
