Languages

Nine languages, one assistant, no favourites.

Parity means every language gets the same capabilities, the same quality bar, and the same release date — verified by a native speaker, not a benchmark. Bhojpuri and Maithili are here on purpose: these are exactly the languages commercial assistants skip.

  • தமிழ்Tamil

    First language of the project. Speech models fine-tuned on Tamil accents; voices reviewed by Tamil stewards.

    Priority one
  • हिन्दीHindi

What a language pack contains

Each language installs as a pack of roughly 600–900MB: fine-tuned speech recognition, two neural voices, a language-filtered knowledge base, translated form templates, and UI strings. Packs are signed, verified before install, and always downloaded with your explicit permission. You can install one language or all nine, and switch between them mid-conversation.

A pack cannot ship until its language steward — a native speaker — has reviewed the strings, the prompts, the voice quality, and the recognition accuracy on fifty real utterances. No machine translation ships unreviewed.
